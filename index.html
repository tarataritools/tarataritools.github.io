About TaraTariTools

TaraTariTools is a personal catalog of free software. Every tool is hand-picked and free to use. The goal is simple: help you find better software without the noise.

You will find privacy-first browsers, open source creative apps, Windows optimization tools and clean installation ISOs here. All links go straight to the source. No mirrors, no bundles, no surprises.

TaraTariTools helps you speed up Windows. Pick your Windows version, download a playbook and follow the steps. The playbook removes bloat and improves performance.
